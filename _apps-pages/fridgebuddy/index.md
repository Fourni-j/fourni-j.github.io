faq_title: Frequently Asked Questions
faq:
- question: How does FridgeBuddy help reduce food waste?
  answer: FridgeBuddy keeps expiration dates visible, sends reminders before food
    expires, and shows consumption and waste patterns. It cannot decide whether food
    is safe to eat; always follow the product label and relevant local food-safety
    guidance.
- question: Can I share my pantry and fridge inventory with my household?
  answer: Yes. A household invite gives family members or roommates access to shared
    storage locations and shopping lists through iCloud.
- question: How do I add food to FridgeBuddy?
  answer: You can scan a barcode, scan a printed expiration date, reuse product details
    stored on your device, or enter an item manually.
- question: Which devices can I use?
  answer: FridgeBuddy works on iPhone with iOS 18 or later and iPad with iPadOS 18
    or later. iCloud keeps your inventory in sync across those devices.
- question: Is FridgeBuddy free?
  answer: FridgeBuddy is free to download and includes in-app purchases for optional
    premium access. Current offers and local pricing are shown on the App Store.
