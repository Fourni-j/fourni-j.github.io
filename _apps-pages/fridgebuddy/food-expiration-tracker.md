## The useful reminder starts when you add the item

Many food trackers fail at the first step: entering a product takes longer than putting it away. FridgeBuddy reduces that friction by letting you scan the barcode for product information and scan the printed expiration date instead of typing both manually.

Products that are not available online can be saved on your device after you enter their details once. The next time the same barcode appears in your kitchen, you can reuse that information rather than starting from zero.

That makes FridgeBuddy practical for regular grocery trips, not only for the occasional item you are particularly worried about.

## See what expires next without doing date math

An inventory becomes useful when it answers a simple question: **what should I use first?** FridgeBuddy groups items into smart lists such as expiring soon and expired, while search and filters help you narrow the list by product or storage location.

You can track food in the:

- Refrigerator
- Freezer
- Pantry or cupboards
- Custom storage locations you create

This gives you one view of time-sensitive food even when it is spread across the kitchen.

## Choose reminder timing that fits the food

Different groceries need different planning windows. A reminder for tonight's leftovers is not the same as a reminder for something stored in the freezer. FridgeBuddy lets you decide how early notifications should arrive, so the alert can prompt an action rather than simply announce that it is too late.

A useful starting routine is:

1. Track the foods you most often forget.
2. Choose enough notice to plan how you will use each one.
3. Check the expiring-soon list before shopping or planning meals.
4. Mark food consumed or wasted so the inventory stays trustworthy.

You do not need to catalog the whole kitchen on day one. A smaller accurate inventory is more valuable than a complete list you stop maintaining.

## Barcode scanning and date scanning solve different jobs

The barcode identifies the product. The printed date tells FridgeBuddy when that particular item needs attention. Using both means the app can fill in reusable details while keeping the date specific to the package in front of you.

FridgeBuddy also supports GS1 Data Matrix barcodes when the code itself includes an expiration date. When that information is present and readable, a single scan can reduce another entry step.

## What FridgeBuddy does—and what it does not do

FridgeBuddy records the date you provide, organizes it, and reminds you. It does not determine whether a product is safe to eat and it does not replace the instructions on the label.

> “Best before,” “use by,” and similar labels can have different meanings. Always follow the product label and the official food-safety guidance that applies where you live.

The app is designed to improve visibility and planning, not to make food-safety decisions on your behalf.

## More than a list of dates

Expiration tracking connects to the rest of the household routine. You can share inventory through iCloud, move needed products into a shopping list, view expiring items in Home Screen widgets, and track what was consumed versus wasted.
