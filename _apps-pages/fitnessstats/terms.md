It does not record workouts itself: each figure is only as accurate as the device or app that recorded the workout, and it only covers the data you allow FitnessStats to read. ## Your Health data

- FitnessStats only asks for permission to **read** your workouts and related data in Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health and cannot change or delete anything there.
- Your Health data is processed on your iPhone. The app keeps a copy of your workouts on the device, shared only with its widgets, so that your stats open quickly. FitnessStats never syncs this copy to iCloud or sends it to us. Like any app data, it is included in your iPhone backups if you make them.
- Otherwise, your Health data leaves the app only when you choose to share or export it, as described below.
