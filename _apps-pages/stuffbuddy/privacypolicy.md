**Last updated:** November 22, 2026 · [Version française](/stuffbuddy/fr/confidentialite.html) · [Deutsche Version](/stuffbuddy/de/datenschutz.html)

This privacy policy describes how the StuffBuddy app, with its widget and Siri and Spotlight features ("StuffBuddy", "the app"), handles your information. StuffBuddy is developed by Charles-Adrien Fournier, an independent developer based in Paris, France, who is responsible for the data processing described here. You can reach me at [support@cafournier.com](mailto:support@cafournier.com).

The short version: your inventory belongs to you. It is stored on your device and in your private iCloud space, where I cannot see it. StuffBuddy has no user accounts and no server of its own, uses no analytics, shows no ads, does not track you across apps and does not sell personal data. To work, it relies on a few services, each listed below with the data it receives.

## Data you add to the app

The things you record in StuffBuddy (items, rooms and containers, photos, receipts, manuals, prices, purchase and warranty details, serial numbers, barcodes, notes, and loans with the name of the person you lent to) are stored:

- **On your device**, so the app works offline.
- **In your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit)**, when you are signed in to iCloud, so your inventory syncs across your Apple devices and stays available if you change devices. This data is tied to your Apple Account and is not accessible to me. If you are not signed in to iCloud, it stays on your device.

## Shared homes

If you share your home, everything it holds (items, places, photos, receipts, manuals, loans and warranties) is shared through iCloud sharing with the people who join. Anyone who has the invitation link can join, and everyone who joins can see and change the whole home, so only send the link to people you trust. To show who added an item and to sum up what the others changed, the app shows the name iCloud gives for each person in the home, or their iCloud email address or phone number when iCloud gives no name.

You can stop sharing your home, or leave a home shared with you, at any time from Settings › Household in the app. When someone leaves, their own home, set aside while they were in the shared one, comes back as it was.

## Photo and receipt analysis

When you add items from a photo of a shelf, or read a receipt, the photo or document is analyzed by Apple Intelligence on Apple's [Private Cloud Compute](https://security.apple.com/blog/private-cloud-compute/), which Apple designed so that your data is used only to answer the request and is neither stored nor made accessible to Apple. The image is processed by Apple, never sent to me or to any other service, and I do not keep it. The photo and receipt themselves are then kept with your inventory as described above.

## Manuals and web links

A manual is kept as a link. When you open it, or use "Find it online" to search for one, the page opens in your browser, with the search engine chosen on your device, and the site you visit receives your request under its own privacy policy. StuffBuddy itself sends nothing to these sites.

## Purchases

StuffBuddy is free to download and offers optional subscriptions and a lifetime purchase, paid through Apple's App Store. Payment details never reach me: Apple processes all transactions.

To know which purchases you are entitled to, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (products, dates, renewals, cancellations and refunds), an anonymous identifier the app creates on first launch, and technical details such as the app version, iOS version and App Store country. RevenueCat is the only third-party service built into the app.

If you also use FridgeBuddy, my other app, it can leave a note on your device that you subscribe to it, with the date, so that StuffBuddy can show its subscribers a special price. StuffBuddy reads this note on your device only; it is not sent anywhere.

## No analytics, no ads, no tracking

StuffBuddy sends no usage analytics, contains no advertising or tracking SDK, uses no advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Notifications, widget, Siri and Spotlight

- **Notifications** (warranty reminders, loan reminders, and the summary of what others changed in a shared home) are scheduled and shown by the app on your device. They do not send your data to a server.
- **The Warranties widget** reads the warranties of the home shown from a storage space on your device that the app shares only with its widget.
- **Siri and Spotlight** can find your items because the app adds them to your device's search index. The index stays on your device and is managed by iOS.

## Support emails

When you contact support from the app, your email app sends the message from your own address. I receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you.

## Data export and deletion

You can export your inventory, or some of its rooms, to a PDF from Settings › Inventory › Export as PDF; the PDF is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Deleting an item, a container or a room removes it from your device and from iCloud, and from the devices of everyone in the home if it is shared. To delete all of StuffBuddy's iCloud data, open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then iCloud › Manage Storage (or Storage), choose StuffBuddy and delete its data. Deleting the app removes the data kept on your device.

## Who receives data

- **Apple** (iCloud, App Store, Private Cloud Compute), under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **RevenueCat, Inc.** (United States): purchase history, anonymous identifier and technical details, stored in the United States. These transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **The people you share your home with**, through iCloud sharing, as described above.

## Legal bases and retention

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the features you use (sync, sharing, photo and receipt analysis, purchases) rely on providing the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b)). Support emails rely on my legitimate interest in answering what you choose to send me (Article 6(1)(f)).

Your inventory stays in your device and iCloud until you delete it. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet accounting obligations. Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request and its follow-ups. Any of this data is deleted on request, within the limits of legal obligations.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to [support@cafournier.com](mailto:support@cafournier.com); I reply within one month. Your inventory is in your device and your iCloud, under your control: you can change or delete it from the app, or from iOS Settings as described above.

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country.

## Children

StuffBuddy is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.
