## Use of the app

StuffBuddy helps you keep an inventory of your home: the things you own, where they are, what they cost, their receipts, manuals and warranties, and what you lent to whom. The app requires no account. Your inventory is stored on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud space. See the [Privacy Policy](/stuffbuddy/privacypolicy.html) for how your information is handled.

## Shared homes

With StuffBuddy Pro, you can share your home with other people through Apple's iCloud sharing. Everyone who joins a shared home can see, add, change and delete everything it holds, including photos, receipts, prices and loans. Anyone who has the invitation link can join, so only send it to people you trust. As the owner, you can stop sharing at any time; people you invited can leave at any time.

## Photo and receipt analysis

StuffBuddy Pro can list the objects in a photo of a shelf and read the store, date, prices and warranty on a receipt. This uses Apple Intelligence on Apple's Private Cloud Compute and is subject to Apple's availability and usage limits: an analysis may be unavailable, or limited for a few hours. The results are suggestions made by a language model. They can be incomplete or wrong, so check them before saving, especially prices and dates.
