## Featured

### [FridgeBuddy — Pantry & Fridge](/fridgebuddy/)

Track your pantry, fridge, and freezer, scan barcodes and expiration dates, share one inventory with your household, and get reminders before food goes to waste. Featured in Apple's "The Best Apps for Parents" story.

Learn more: [food expiration tracking](/fridgebuddy/food-expiration-tracker/), [pantry inventory](/fridgebuddy/pantry-inventory-app/), [household sharing](/fridgebuddy/shared-pantry-app/), [pantry app comparison](/fridgebuddy/alternatives/), or try the [food-waste calculator](/fridgebuddy/food-waste-calculator/).
